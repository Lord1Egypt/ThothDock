Connect this browser

Pair once with the code shown in the ThothDock app on your phone (Web Panel page).

  1. Check the address. It must start with https://.
  2. Check the certificate. Your browser warns that it is self-signed. Open the certificate details and compare the SHA-256 fingerprint with the one in the app. Continue only if they match.
  3. Enter the code. It works once and expires after 10 minutes. Ask the app for a new one if it ran out.
